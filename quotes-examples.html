Quotes Examples

Direct quotes from the artists, songwriters and producers, pulled from the Songfacts and American Songwriter interview archives.

“The song is really fun, but I can remember those feelings of betrayal.”

“

“I think that I'm known for my vulnerability. I didn't ask for that. I didn't ask for some of the stories that have brought me success.”

“When I close my eyes, I imagine a riot going on when I hear this riff.”

“I wanted to talk about the power of my girlfriend's very small isms, little things that she does that she doesn't realize are quite powerful to the people around her, generosity and care, and all those things.”

“Instinctively, musicians are lazy. They are some of the laziest people in the world.”
