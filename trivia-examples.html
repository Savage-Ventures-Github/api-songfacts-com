Trivia Examples

Songfacts has been writing trivia questions in various forms for decades – we know how to make them compelling. Trivia is customized for each client, but here are some examples:

What male solo artist had #1 Hot 100 hits in the 1990s, 2000s, and 2010s?

Response »

Usher had nine #1 hits, starting with “Nice & Slow” in 1998 and ending with “OMG” in 2010.

Play Usher:

What actress who has starred in movies with John Malkovich and Will Ferrell had a Top 10 album in 2010 as part of a duo?

Response »

You heard her sing in the movie ‘Elf,’ but Zooey Deschanel is also a recording artist. She and M. Ward formed the duo She & Him in 2008; their album Volume Two went to #6 in 2010.

Play She & Him:

What B-52's song inspired the title of a '90s movie?

Response »

“Private Idaho” provided the title to the 1991 movie My Own Private Idaho, starring River Phoenix and Keanu Reeves.

Play “Private Idaho”
